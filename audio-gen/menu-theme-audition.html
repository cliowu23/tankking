TanKING — Menu Theme Audition

Menu-music palette / archive. M3 "Signal Home" is now the live menu theme. M1 "Long Road" and the old menu theme are saved here as parked alternates (M2 was dropped). One plays at a time, each loops (give each ~20s — they bloom in). Use headphones.
All A minor (Am–F–C–G), first drafts, all tunable — tempo, how lush, how prominent the melody. Whatever you pick gets ported into MusicManager._build_menu().
